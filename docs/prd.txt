1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
1.1 Product Description
DIOKS FashionSuite is a cloud-based fashion business management application designed to help fashion professionals organize, protect and manage important business information.
The first version (MVP) will focus primarily on providing a digital measurement book, customer management, customer project records and convenient WhatsApp Business access.
The long-term vision is to develop a comprehensive management platform serving different sectors of the fashion industry.
1.2 Problem Statement
Many fashion professionals depend on physical measurement books, notebooks and mobile-phone notes to store customer information.
These records can be lost, damaged or misplaced, resulting in the loss of valuable customer measurements and business information.
DIOKS FashionSuite will provide a secure digital alternative that allows users to store, retrieve and manage their records across devices.
1.3 Product Vision
To become a comprehensive digital management platform for the fashion industry.
2. PRODUCT GOALS AND OBJECTIVES
The objectives of DIOKS FashionSuite are to:
Provide a reliable digital measurement book.
Protect customer measurements and business records from loss.
Improve customer information management.
Organize customer projects and job details.
Support convenient customer communication through WhatsApp Business.
Allow users to recover their records when changing or losing devices.
Establish a foundation for a complete fashion business management system.
3. TARGET USERS
The platform is intended to accommodate:
Independent tailors.
Fashion designers.
Bespoke fashion businesses.
Ready-to-wear designers.
Clothing manufacturers.
Mass-production businesses.
School-uniform producers.
Boutiques and fashion retailers.
Fashion wholesalers.
MVP approach: The application should accommodate different categories of fashion businesses, while its first release concentrates on the agreed core measurement-book features.
4. MVP SCOPE – FIRST RELEASE
The first release will include:

Customer project management.

5. USER REGISTRATION AND BUSINESS PROFILE
5.1 Account Creation
Users shall be able to:
Register using an email address.
Create a username and password.
Recover forgotten passwords.
Log in securely.
Access their accounts from supported devices.
Google sign-in may also be offered.
5.2 Business Profile
Each user shall create a business profile containing:
Business or brand name.
Business logo.
Business phone number.
WhatsApp number.
Email address.
Social media accounts.
Business address, where applicable.
Company registration information, where applicable.
Both registered and unregistered businesses must be accommodated.
6. CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT
6.1 Customer List
The application shall provide a customer list displaying:
Customer name.
Customer photograph, where available.
Phone number.
Search functionality.
Add New Customer option.
6.2 Individual Customer Profile
Selecting a customer shall open their individual profile.
The profile shall contain:
A. Customer Information
Full name.
Phone number.
Email address.
Photograph.
Birthday, if provided.
Wedding anniversary, if provided.
B. Customer Measurements
A dedicated section containing the customer's saved measurements.
C. Customer Projects
A dedicated section containing the customer's current, pending, completed and archived projects.
6.3 Customer Celebrations
Where customers voluntarily provide their birthdays or wedding anniversaries, the system should support reminders and, where technically supported and authorized, automated celebratory messages.
7. DIGITAL MEASUREMENT BOOK
7.1 Standard Measurement Template
The application shall provide an initial standard measurement template.
7.2 Customizable Measurements
Users shall be able to:
Add measurement fields.
Edit measurement labels.
Rename measurement terms.
Remove unnecessary fields.
Customize the template according to their working methods.
7.3 Measurement History
The system should preserve previous measurement versions when measurements are updated.
Users should be able to retrieve and compare previous measurements.
7.4 Measurement Retrieval
Users shall be able to retrieve saved customer measurements whenever the customer returns.
8. CUSTOMER PROJECT MANAGEMENT
8.1 Project Creation
Each customer shall have the ability to own multiple projects.
A project shall function as a work diary for a particular customer order.
8.2 Project Information
Each project shall contain:
Project name.
Customer identification.
Linked customer measurements.
Design photograph or reference image.
Job description.
Customer instructions.
Special notes.
Order received date.
Expected delivery date.
Collection or delivery date.
Relevant WhatsApp messages or attachments, where manually saved.
8.3 Project Status
Projects shall support distinct statuses, including:
Pending.
In Progress.
Completed.
Delivered.
Archived.
8.4 Project Completion and Delivery
The tailor shall manually mark a project as completed.
Completed and delivered must remain separate statuses.
Marking a project as delivered shall trigger a confirmation prompt.
After confirmation, the application shall remind the tailor to archive the project.
Delivery dates shall trigger reminders but shall not automatically archive projects.
Archived projects shall remain accessible under the customer's profile.
9. WHATSAPP BUSINESS INTEGRATION
9.1 WhatsApp Business Requirement
Every tailor should be encouraged to establish a WhatsApp Business account for professional customer communication and brand promotion.
9.2 WhatsApp Access
DIOKS FashionSuite shall provide a clearly visible WhatsApp icon.
Selecting the icon should open the tailor's WhatsApp Business application.
9.3 Customer Communication
Customers may communicate with tailors using either:
Regular WhatsApp.
WhatsApp Business.
Customers shall not be required to install DIOKS FashionSuite.
9.4 Design Sharing
Users shall be able to share design photographs and project references directly from FashionSuite to WhatsApp Business.
9.5 Conversation Storage
WhatsApp shall remain the primary location for storing WhatsApp conversations.
Messages shall not automatically be copied into FashionSuite under the initial agreed approach.
Important messages may be manually saved to the relevant customer project.
9.6 Future Integration
Official Meta WhatsApp Business Platform integration may be considered in a future phase if automatic message collection becomes a priority.
10. SECURITY, CLOUD STORAGE AND DATA PROTECTION
10.1 Secure Cloud Storage
All customer records, measurements, photographs, projects and other saved information shall be stored securely in the cloud.
10.2 Account Recovery
Users shall be able to recover forgotten passwords and access their information on replacement devices.
10.3 Data Privacy
Each tailor shall have a private account.
One user must not be able to access another user's customer records, measurements or projects.
10.4 Subscription Expiry
Subscription expiry shall restrict access to the application and its stored business records.
Subscription expiry must never automatically delete user data.
Following successful renewal, access shall be restored with all previous records intact.
10.5 Data Ownership
Users shall be able to:
Delete their own records.
Export or download their business information.
Manage their customer records.
10.6 Backup and Recovery
The system shall implement secure backup and recovery procedures to protect against accidental data loss and system failure.
11. SUBSCRIPTION AND PAYMENT MANAGEMENT
11.1 MVP Subscription Structure
The MVP shall initially operate with one subscription package.
All eligible users shall have access to the same agreed core features.
11.2 Free Trial
New users shall receive a 14-day free trial.
11.3 Future Subscription Tiers
The subscription architecture shall support future packages, including:
Package
Proposed Coverage

Professional
Regular features plus accounting and transaction records
Business
Professional features plus invoicing, receipts and additional business tools
Enterprise
Advanced management tools for larger fashion businesses
These packages will be introduced as their corresponding features become available.
11.4 Subscription Flexibility
The system shall support:
Different subscription prices.
Different feature permissions.
Subscription upgrades.
Subscription downgrades.
Subscription renewal.
Payment confirmation.
11.5 Data Protection During Subscription Changes
Changes to subscription status must not automatically delete customer information or business records.
12. ADMINISTRATIVE MANAGEMENT
The platform shall provide an administrative dashboard.
Administrators shall be able to:
Manage user accounts.
Monitor subscription activities.
Manage payment records.
Configure subscription packages.
Manage free-trial settings.
Monitor platform activities.
Handle customer support requests.
Monitor suspicious activities and platform abuse.
Administrative access must follow strict permissions.
Administrators should not have unrestricted access to users' private customer measurements, photographs or project information.

14. MVP SUCCESS CRITERIA
The first version shall be considered successful when the following objectives are achieved:
Users can create accounts and business profiles successfully.
Users can save and retrieve customer measurements.
Users can retrieve customer information after changing or losing their phones.
Password and account recovery work successfully.
Users can customize measurement templates.
Users can create and manage customer projects.
Users can share project photographs through WhatsApp Business.
Project reminders and archiving work as specified.
Subscription expiry does not result in data deletion.
Successful subscription renewal restores access to previous records.
At least three pilot tailors test the application and confirm that it helps reduce the risk of losing measurements compared with physical notebooks.
At least 80% of pilot users can create a customer project within 10 minutes without assistance.
15. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
The application shall be designed with the following qualities:
Cross-platform compatibility: Android and iOS support.
Security: Protection against unauthorized access.
Reliability: Consistent storage and retrieval of records.
Usability: Simple navigation suitable for everyday tailoring work.
Performance: Responsive screens and efficient data retrieval.
Scalability: Ability to support increasing numbers of users and future modules.
Maintainability: Ability to introduce additional features without rebuilding the entire application.

17. CONCLUSION
DIOKS FashionSuite is designed to address a practical problem within the fashion industry: the loss, disorganization and difficulty of managing valuable customer and business information.
The initial MVP will establish a secure digital measurement book and customer management system while creating a scalable foundation for a much broader fashion business management platform.
The product will grow progressively, introducing additional tools and subscription packages as new capabilities become available.
Product principle: Build the measurement book first. Build the complete fashion business management platform around it.
What happens after the PRD?
The PRD is the document that explains what we are building and why. It is not yet the complete technical instruction for developers.
I recommend this next sequence:
PRD review and approval: Check that the requirements accurately reflect your vision.
SRS – Software Requirements Specification: Convert the agreed requirements into detailed instructions that developers can implement and test.
UI/UX design: Design the actual screens and how users move through the application.
Database design: Plan how customer records, measurements, projects and other information will be stored.
Software architecture: Decide how the Android and iOS applications, backend and cloud services will work together.
Development and testing: Build and test the MVP.
My recommendation: Before handing this to a developer or investor, we should review and approve the PRD together. That will help prevent misunderstandings, unnecessary development costs and features being built differently from what you intended.
