SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION

1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the functional and non-functional requirements for the development of DIOKS FashionSuite.

It provides developers, designers, testers and other stakeholders with a clear understanding of the software's expected behavior, features, performance and security requirements.

The document will serve as a technical reference throughout the design, development, testing and deployment of the application.

1.2 Product Scope

DIOKS FashionSuite is a digital fashion business management application designed to help fashion professionals manage customer information, body measurements, project records and business communication.

The initial release (MVP) will focus on:

1. User registration and account management.
2. Business profile management.
3. Customer registration and management.
4. Digital customer measurement records.
5. Customizable measurement templates.
6. Customer project management.
7. Project status tracking and archiving.
8. WhatsApp Business access.
9. Secure cloud storage and data recovery.
10. Subscription and payment management.
11. Administrative management.

Additional modules, including accounting, inventory management, invoicing and receipts, will be introduced in future development phases.

1.3 Intended Audience

This document is intended for:

- Software developers.
- UI/UX designers.
- Software testers.
- Project managers.
- System administrators.
- Business owners.
- Potential investors and technical partners.

1.4 Product Objectives

The system shall:

1. Provide a reliable digital alternative to physical measurement books.
2. Allow tailors to maintain individual customer profiles.
3. Store and retrieve customer measurements securely.
4. Allow multiple projects under one customer profile.
5. Maintain project history and status.
6. Provide convenient WhatsApp Business access.
7. Support secure account access across multiple devices.
8. Protect customer information against unauthorized access and accidental loss.
9. Provide a scalable foundation for future fashion business management features.

1.6 Product Development Principle

DIOKS FashionSuite shall be developed using a modular and scalable architecture.

The initial release shall prioritize the digital measurement book and customer management functions while allowing future modules to be added without requiring a complete redesign of the application.
